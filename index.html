What it actually does

Everything below is in the app you are two clicks from. No tiers, no upsell.

Four views, one grid

Month, week, day and agenda. Drag a chip to another day, click a slot to create, arrow keys to move.

Tasks that close

Lists, due dates, notes, drag to reorder, a Dates view that groups everything by when it's owed. Dated tasks show on the calendar.

Google, both ways

Calendar and Tasks pull and push, on a timer, on tab focus, and after every edit. Or import an .ics and stay offline.

Yours, locally

Your calendars are Google calendars, so your events sync themselves between devices. No Playday server, no analytics, nothing of ours in the middle.

The honest bits

  • It's one HTML file, one stylesheet, one script. No framework, no build step.
  • Signing in is required once a Google Client ID is configured — the calendars are the storage.
  • Sound and confetti are synthesised at runtime — there isn't one audio file in the repo.
  • Google sync needs your own OAuth Client ID. That's the price of no backend.
  • Data is per-browser. Two tabs will fight; last write wins.
  • Undo covers deletes. It doesn't cover edits.
  • Installable on Chrome, Edge, and iOS via Share → Add to Home Screen.
